RadioSee privacy and data use

Updated October 5, 2026

Sensing and network data

RadioSee receives sensing frames from the service address you configure. On Mac, Wi-Fi scans include nearby network names, network identifiers, channels and signal measurements. These are sent to the configured sensing service only when scan sharing is enabled. A remote address sends data to that remote server; local-only processing depends on choosing a local service.

What is saved on this device

The app saves room names, node placement assignments, presence and activity events, minute-level motion and confidence samples, your feedback, and reminder preferences in its local application storage. Records are kept for 1, 7 or 30 days and pruned while the app runs. Demo readings are not saved to this activity history. Storage is capped at 5,000 events and 43,200 minute samples; the oldest entries are removed when these limits are exceeded, so actual retention may be shorter than the selected period.

Camera training and server data

Camera access is used only when you start pose training. Training may save pose labels and signal recordings and send training requests to your configured service. Local activity deletion does not delete training recordings or data retained by that server. Manage those files and the server separately.

Permissions and notifications

Local network permission supports service and sensor discovery. macOS location permission allows access to Wi-Fi scan information. Camera permission supports optional pose training. Notification permission is requested only when you enable reminders. Notifications can reveal room names and activity on the lock screen; adjust system notification privacy if needed.

Control and deletion

In My, choose record retention, delete activity records and feedback, or reset room configuration and reminders. Turn off scan sharing in Preferences to stop forwarding nearby Wi-Fi scan data. Server pairing and training storage have separate controls. Settings such as the service address remain until you change them or reset the app's preferences.

External connections

The app does not include advertising or analytics tracking. Opening research, support or legal links connects you to those external websites under their own policies. Your selected sensing server's storage and network behavior are controlled by its operator.

This privacy page

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub may process access logs such as your IP address under its privacy statement. Website access data is separate from sensing records within the app. This page embeds no advertising, analytics scripts or third-party fonts.
