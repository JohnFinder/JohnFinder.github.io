Rosettica Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026 · 中文

Rosettica does not require an account and includes no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. You choose where translation text is processed. No content is sent automatically to a different translation server.

On-device translation

Preparing on-device translation downloads a model from Hugging Face. The provider receives download requests and associated network information. After preparation, text translation runs on your device; your translation text is not sent to the model provider.

Nearby Mac and configured servers

Nearby-Mac mode sends text to the Mac you select on the same network. The current connection uses HTTP with a pairing token and is not encrypted. Use a trusted network. Remote mode sends text to your configured server. Its operator controls processing and retention. HTTPS encrypts the connection; HTTP does not. API keys and pairing credentials are kept in the system Keychain. Endpoint addresses and other preferences are saved locally.

Images and voice

Image text recognition runs locally. Importing a photo can involve the system photo library, including Apple's iCloud Photos if you use it. Apple speech recognition may use online services depending on your device and language. Text-to-speech uses system voices. These services are separate from the chosen text translation backend.

History, preferences and backups

Translation history, saved language combinations, glossary entries and quality baselines are saved locally. You can delete history and disable new history. Optional iCloud backup sends your history to your iCloud account. Deleting local records does not remove a previous backup. A system share extension temporarily saves shared text in a private shared inbox for you to review in Rosettica before translation.

Feedback and measurements

Quality issue reports are saved locally without source text or translations. Optional local task measurements are off by default and include timings, language counts, outcomes and dates, with no text, server addresses or credentials. Nothing is uploaded automatically. You may inspect, export or clear diagnostics. Exported quality evaluations contain the public evaluation samples and their model outputs.

Purchases

Optional tips are handled by Apple's StoreKit and App Store payment services. Rosettica does not receive payment card details.

Your controls

Review the active processing destination before translating, choose whether to keep history, delete local records, manage model files and credentials, and control optional backup and diagnostic exports. Content already sent to a configured server is subject to that server operator's practices. Removing the app does not delete a previously created iCloud backup.

Rosettica 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 5 日

Rosettica 无需注册账户，不包含广告或第三方分析 SDK。你自行选择翻译文本的处理位置。应用不会自动切换翻译服务器。

设备端翻译

准备设备端翻译时，应用会从 Hugging Face 下载模型。模型提供方会收到下载请求和相关网络信息。准备完成后，翻译在你的设备上运行，翻译文本不会发送给模型提供方。

附近 Mac 与自定义服务器

附近 Mac 模式将文本发送到你在同一网络中选择的 Mac。当前连接使用 HTTP 和配对令牌，不进行加密，请使用可信网络。远程模式将文本发送到你配置的服务器，由服务器运营者决定处理和保留方式。HTTPS 可加密连接，HTTP 不会加密。API 密钥与配对凭据保存在系统钥匙串中，服务器地址和其他偏好保存在本机。

图片与语音

图片文字识别在本机运行。导入照片可能涉及系统照片图库及你启用的 Apple iCloud 照片。Apple 语音识别可能根据设备和语言使用在线服务；语音朗读使用系统声音。这些服务与所选的文本翻译后端独立。

历史、偏好与备份

翻译历史、语言组合、术语表和质量基线保存在本机。你可以删除历史并关闭新历史保存。可选的 iCloud 备份会将历史发送到你的 iCloud 账户。删除本地记录不会删除已有备份。系统分享扩展会临时将分享文本保存在私有共享收件箱，供你在 Rosettica 中确认后翻译。

反馈与测量

质量问题反馈保存在本机，不包含原文或译文。可选的本地任务测量默认关闭，仅记录耗时、语言数量、结果和日期，不包含文本、服务器地址或凭据。数据不会自动上传。你可以查看、导出或清除诊断记录。导出的质量评估包含公开评估样本及模型输出。

购买

可选赞赏由 Apple StoreKit 和 App Store 支付服务处理。Rosettica 不会收到银行卡信息。

你的控制与联系

翻译前查看当前处理位置；你可以管理历史、模型文件、凭据、备份和诊断导出。已发送到服务器的内容受其运营者的规则约束。卸载应用不会删除已有的 iCloud 备份。
