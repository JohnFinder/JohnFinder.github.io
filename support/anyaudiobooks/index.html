Getting started

  1. Import a document or paste text, review the extracted content, then save it.
  2. Open the document and choose a system voice to begin listening.
  3. Use the chapter list, bookmarks, and saved reading position to return to a passage.
  4. For local speech models or document translation, download the required resources first. Available models and languages depend on your device and the selected model.

Common questions

My scanned PDF has no selectable text

Scanned pages need text recognition. Check the extracted text before saving. Image quality and the document language affect recognition accuracy.

A local model is downloading slowly

Check your connection and available device storage. Model files can be large. Use the available download controls to pause or resume. You can continue using an available system voice while preparing local models.

A configured speech service or node does not connect

Confirm the server address and credentials with its operator and check that the service is reachable from your device. The developer does not operate public speech services, book-sharing nodes, or voice marketplaces. Independent providers may retain the content you send to them; review their policies before uploading. Do not send service keys to support.

My voice reference does not produce the expected result

Use a clear recording that you have permission to use. Provide an accurate transcript when the selected model requires one. Language support and reference requirements vary by model. Preview the voice before preparing a full book.

How do I manage storage?

Review storage and downloaded models in the app. Prepared audio can use substantial space. Keep a separate copy of original material you need before deleting it.

How do I troubleshoot iCloud?

Confirm that your devices use the intended Apple account and that iCloud is available. Enable optional sync in the app and review its status. Allow time for uploads and synchronization to finish.

How do I delete content sent to an external service?

Contact the service operator about retained uploads or logs. Removing a local item or disconnecting a service does not delete copies already held by that provider or recipients. Public ledger records may not be erasable.
